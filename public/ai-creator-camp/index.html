Why now

The gap between a creative degree and a creative job just moved.

The brief changed

Agencies now expect a junior to arrive with prompt craft, AI image and video workflows, and the judgement to art-direct a machine. Most curricula have not caught up.

The portfolio changed

A student who can show a finished campaign film they made alone in a week stands apart from a student showing mood boards. AI collapsed the gap between idea and artefact.

The hiring changed

We hire on demonstrated output, not transcripts. A competition day gives us a real look at how a student thinks under a deadline — and gives them a direct route in.

The proposal

One programme, two parts.

Learn it, then prove it. The camp builds the skill; the festival tests it in public, under a real brief, in front of a jury.

Part one

AI Creator Camp

Sixty per cent of every session is the student's hands on the tool. Delivered by Paralox Media with Cocoon.

01

Creative direction with AI

Reading a brief, building a visual thesis, and directing a model instead of accepting its first answer. Prompt craft, reference building, holding a brand consistent across dozens of outputs.

02

Image, identity and key visuals

Campaign key visuals, product imagery and identity systems built with generative tools, then finished properly — retouching, typography, layout and export discipline.

03

AI video production

Script to storyboard to shot list to cut. Generating and directing sequences, matching continuity, sound design, grade, and knowing when a real camera is still the right call.

04

Shipping and responsibility

Packaging work for a client and a portfolio, pricing your time, disclosure and rights, and the ethical lines an agency will hold you to on day one.

Format

Four half-day sessions, one per week, on the AMDT campus. Live demo, guided build, critique.

Outcome

Every student leaves with four finished pieces and a portfolio-ready case study of their own work.

Recognition

A certificate of completion co-signed by AMDT School of Creativity and Paralox Media.

Part two

AIdeaFest

One day. One live brief. Teams build a complete campaign from nothing and pitch it to a jury before the doors close.

Run of show

09:00
Brief drop. A live, client-style brief written by Paralox and sealed until the room is seated.
09:30
Case study: leaders in creative AI. A teardown of real campaigns — what the brief was, what the AI actually did, what a human had to fix.
10:30
Build sprint opens. Teams of three. Concept, key visual, and a short film.
13:00
Lunch and mentor floor. Paralox and Cocoon mentors move between teams on request.
14:30
Checkpoint critique. Ten minutes per team, honest and fast, to catch a team going the wrong way.
16:30
Submissions close. Hard cut-off, as it would be with a client.
17:00
Pitches. Five minutes to present, three to defend, in front of the full room.
18:30
Awards. Prizes, internship offers and closing remarks.

Commercials

Three ways to structure it.

Pick the model that fits AMDT's academic calendar and budget. A costed breakdown follows within five working days of the model being chosen.

A

Sponsored programme

Paralox Media funds delivery, prize pool and production in exchange for headline partner billing across the programme, on-campus visibility and first access to graduating talent. No cost to AMDT beyond venue and coordination.

B

Cost-shared

Delivery costs split between AMDT and Paralox Media, with the prize pool carried by Paralox. Both partners share billing equally and co-own all programme content.

C

Per-student fee

A fixed fee per enrolled student covering the four Creator Camp modules, with AIdeaFest run as a free open day for the wider student body. Suited to running the camp as a paid elective.

Whichever model is chosen, the internship offers and the prize pool are carried by Paralox Media. They are not conditional on the commercial structure.
